Where Port Monmouth sits

Port Monmouth is an unincorporated community in the northwest part of Middletown Township, on the Raritan Bayshore. Its northern edge is the shore of Sandy Hook Bay and Route 36 is its southern border. Belford lies to the east and North Middletown to the west. Tidal Compton Creek runs along the east side and Pews Creek along the west. The ZIP code is 07758.

Housing and population

The 2020 census counted 3,745 people in 1,396 households and 1,486 housing units, with 6.1 percent vacant. The population was down 1.9 percent from 3,818 in 2010. Port Monmouth first appeared in the 1950 census and became a census-designated place in 1990.

The flood project and what it does not change

A U.S. Army Corps of Engineers project protects the community. A September 2020 Army Corps article described a 15-foot dune about 1.5 miles long, a concrete floodwall along Pews and Compton creeks, a 7,070-foot levee, pump stations and a tide gate at Pews Creek. The same article said Hurricane Sandy destroyed 750 homes and businesses in the area.

That work is built to reduce flooding from the water. It does not change what is inside your pipes or sump pit, and it was still under construction when that article was written. Check current status with the township.

Plumbing checks after a storm or flood

  • Shut off the main water valve if pipes or fixtures are damaged.
  • Do not use a gas appliance or water heater that was under water until a qualified professional has checked it.
  • Do not run a flooded sump pump or electrical equipment while standing in water.
  • Take photos before cleanup and call your insurer.
  • If drains back up, stop using water and call for a sewer line cleaning visit.

Jobs worth a call

Homes near the water often care about sump pump condition, basement waterproofing questions and drain cleaning. A water heater repair call is common after any storm. Ask a plumber whether a backwater valve or battery backup makes sense for your house, because the answer depends on how your system is built.
