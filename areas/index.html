Areas We Serve near Middletown, NJ

Our dispatch line covers all of Middletown Township and nearby towns. Red Bank has its own page because it is a separate borough with its own water system.

☎ Call (732) 466-1098

Where we work

Middletown Township covers about 41 square miles, so we group the neighborhoods you will hear about. Belford, Port Monmouth and Leonardo are bay-side communities. Lincroft is inland, near Thompson Park. Navesink, Fairview, North Middletown and Middletown Village are part of the township too, and all are served by the same dispatch line.

In every town, plumbing is done to the same state standard, the National Standard Plumbing Code 2021, adopted at N.J.A.C. 5:23-3.15, and permits come from the local construction office.

Holmdel is a separate township to the west and is covered by the same dispatch line. Red Bank is a separate borough on the Navesink River with its own water system, so it has a dedicated page below.

Dedicated town page

The borough below has its own water source and rules, so its page explains who to call for what. If you live in another nearby town, call anyway and the dispatcher will tell you whether we cover it.

How coverage works

Dispatch covers Middletown Township, so response is usually quickest inside the township. Traffic on Route 35, Route 36 and the Garden State Parkway, bridge openings, storms and call volume all affect arrival. The dispatcher gives you an estimate when you call and never promises a fixed arrival time. Calling from the road or giving a nearby landmark helps the dispatcher place you on the map quickly, which speeds the dispatch itself.
