Privacy Policy

This site has no forms and does not ask you to type personal information. This page explains what information may still be collected.

☎ Call (732) 466-1098

What we collect

When you call, the number you call from and the details you tell the dispatcher are used to send a plumber and to follow up on the job. We do not sell phone call information.

Call routing or tracking services may handle calls on our behalf. Ask the dispatcher about call recording if it matters to you.

What your browser shares

Like any website, the server and some embedded services receive standard technical data such as your IP address and browser type. This site loads fonts from Google Fonts and may show an embedded Google Maps view of our service area. Those services have their own privacy policies.

Cookies

We do not use this site to build user profiles. Embedded services may set their own cookies, and you can control cookies in your browser settings.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete information you gave us on a call by phoning the dispatch line. This policy may change, and the date at the bottom of the page will show updates.
