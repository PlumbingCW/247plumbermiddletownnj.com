Where Navesink sits

Navesink is an unincorporated community in eastern Middletown Township, south of the borough of Atlantic Highlands. Route 36 runs near its northern border and County Route 516 clips the northern end. Claypit Creek drains south into the tidal Navesink River, and Many Mind Creek flows north. Elevation is about 118 feet.

Housing and mail

The 2020 census counted 2,004 people in 712 households and 765 housing units, with 6.9 percent vacant. Population was down 0.8 percent from 2,020 in 2010. The ZIP code 07752 is for post office boxes, and street addresses use 07716, the Atlantic Highlands ZIP. That is why a Navesink address can show an Atlantic Highlands mailing city. It is still inside Middletown Township.

The historic district

The Navesink Historic District was added to the National Register of Historic Places on September 5, 1975, and has 96 contributing properties, including churches, a school, houses, a library and commercial buildings. The township describes it as centered on Monmouth Avenue and Navesink Avenue, a primarily 19th-century village that grew around a commercial crossroads. In the 19th century it was the area's largest town.

Working on an older building

Not every building in a historic village is old inside, and not every old building has old pipes. Plumbing in a long-lived house has often been changed several times. The honest approach is for a plumber to look first, then explain what is there and what, if anything, needs replacing.

Before any exterior change, such as a new vent or a relocated meter, ask the township construction office whether historic review applies to your property.

Jobs worth a call

Leak detection finds slow leaks without opening walls. Repiping is worth discussing only after an inspection shows a reason. Water heater replacement and plumbing installation are common when a house is updated.
