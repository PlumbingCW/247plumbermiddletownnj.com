Contact Middletown 24/7 Plumbing

The fastest way to reach a plumber is the phone. The dispatch line at (732) 466-1098 is answered 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

☎ Call (732) 466-1098See services

Call, do not type

We do not use contact forms. A form message can sit unread while water spreads, and a phone call reaches dispatch at once. The dispatcher follows written guidelines for what to tell you to shut off while you wait. For an emergency, call (732) 466-1098.

For a non-urgent question, call as well. The dispatcher can book a same-day or next-day visit when available.

Phone and email

Middletown 24/7 Plumbing serves Middletown Township and nearby towns by phone. Email is available at the address in the footer for non-urgent questions.

There is no walk-in office, so please call before you plan a visit.

What to tell the dispatcher

Have these ready when you call

  • Your street address and nearest cross street.
  • What is happening, in plain words, and whether water is still running.
  • Whether you have already closed the main valve.
  • Whether the property is a house, apartment, shop or office.
  • A good number to reach you if the call drops.
