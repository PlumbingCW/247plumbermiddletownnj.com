Where Belford sits

Belford is an unincorporated community along the northern edge of Middletown Township, on the south shore of Sandy Hook Bay. Port Monmouth lies to the west and Leonardo to the east, and Route 36 forms its southern border. It has Belford Harbor and a tidal marsh along Ware Creek in the east. The ZIP code is 07718.

A small community built around the water

The 2020 census counted 1,648 people in Belford, down 6.8 percent from 1,768 in 2010. The 2010 census counted 616 housing units. Belford was counted as a census-designated place beginning in 2000.

The Belford ferry slip is NY Waterway's Raritan Bayshore terminal, with a crossing of about 50 minutes to New York City and Exchange Place in Jersey City. Commuting means many households are out of the house for long stretches, and that shapes the plumbing advice below.

When the house is empty for a day or a week

A leak is worse when nobody is home to see it. A few habits help:

  • Know where the main shutoff is, and close it before a long trip.
  • Check washing machine hoses and the area around the water heater for drips.
  • Look at your water meter when no water is running. If it moves, something is leaking.
  • In freezing weather, keep the heat on and open cabinet doors under sinks.
  • Ask a plumber about a leak sensor near the water heater and under sinks.

Jobs worth a call

Leak detection is the right call for a meter that moves or a bill that jumps. If a pipe does fail, burst pipe repair comes first. Other common visits are water heater repair and faucet repair.
