About Middletown 24/7 Plumbing

Middletown 24/7 Plumbing is a call-only plumbing dispatch service for Middletown, NJ and nearby towns. This page explains how a call works, what we promise and what we do not.

☎ Call (732) 466-1098

How a call works

You call (732) 466-1098 and reach dispatch. Tell the dispatcher your address, the symptom and whether water is still running. They tell you what to shut off, then send the next available plumber.

There are no forms and no booking portal. Everything starts with a phone call, which is faster when water is on the floor.

What we stand behind

We tell you the price before work starts, and we explain what moves it. We offer same-day appointments when available. New Jersey requires a licensed master plumber for plumbing work. Our work follows the National Standard Plumbing Code as adopted in the state.

We do not publish license numbers, named individuals or years in business on this site. If you want to confirm credentials, ask the dispatcher and verify them with the state.

What we do not do

We do not drywall, paint or restore flooring after water damage. For that, a restoration company is the right call, and your insurer may have preferred vendors. We also do not guess at prices over the phone for complex jobs, because a pipe inside a wall needs a look first.

How we decide what to recommend

A good plumber explains the problem before the price. When a repair will hold, we say so. When a pipe, tank or line is near the end of its life, we say that too and explain why. The aim is a fix that fits the house and the budget, not the biggest invoice.

You can ask to see the failed part, the camera footage or a photo of the problem. That is a normal request, and a plumber who explains what you are looking at is easy to trust.

The towns and neighborhoods we cover

Dispatch covers all of Middletown Township, which stretches about 41 square miles from Raritan Bay and Sandy Hook Bay to the Navesink River and the inland streets around Lincroft. Belford, Port Monmouth, Leonardo, Navesink, Fairview, North Middletown and Middletown Village are all inside the township. Holmdel and Red Bank are nearby and are served as well.

Each area has its own plumbing history. Bay-side streets were flooded by Sandy in October 2012, and many homes were repaired or raised. Older streets near Middletown Village have homes built long before modern plumbing codes. Knowing which kind of house you have helps the dispatcher send the right parts.

Licensing and code

New Jersey requires plumbing work to be done by a licensed master plumber under N.J.S.A. 45:14C-1, and the plumbing subcode is the National Standard Plumbing Code 2021, adopted at N.J.A.C. 5:23-3.15. Permits and inspections are handled by the local construction office. In Middletown that is the office at 1 Kings Highway, 732-615-2106.

We cannot list a license number on this site, and we do not make claims we cannot back. Ask the dispatcher about credentials, and check them with the state if you want to confirm.

How pricing works

A clear price is given before work starts. The plumber looks at the problem, explains the options and tells you what each costs. If the job changes after a wall is opened, the plumber stops and explains before going on. You are never asked to approve work you have not heard described.

Cost depends on the problem, the access, the parts and the time of day. After-hours emergency calls cost more than scheduled weekday visits, because a crew is held on call for them. Our service pages list the cost factors for each kind of job, so you can see what moves a price before you call.

Who the dispatch line is for

Homeowners, renters, landlords, shop owners and property managers all use the same line. Tell the dispatcher which one you are, because access, approval and billing differ. A renter may need the landlord's permission, and a manager may need a work order. Saying so on the first call saves a second one.

Safety first

If you smell gas, leave the building and call the gas utility or 911 before you call a plumber. If water is near electrical equipment, stay clear. If sewage is backing up, stop using water and keep children and pets away. These steps matter more than any plumbing fix.

Where we are

We take calls for Middletown Township and nearby towns. There is no walk-in office, so please call (732) 466-1098 before you go anywhere.
