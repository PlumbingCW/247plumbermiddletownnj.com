Emergency Faucet Repair in Middletown, NJ

Faucet repair in Middletown, NJ covers dripping spouts, leaking handles, shower and tub valves, broken supply lines and fixtures that will not shut off. A burst supply line or a valve that will not close is an emergency, and our dispatch line is open 24 hours a day.

For a gushing fixture, shut the small valve under the sink or the main valve and call. That limits the water until the plumber arrives.

Faucet emergency: find the right shutoff first

Under almost every sink are two small oval-handle valves. Turning them clockwise stops hot and cold water to that faucet. For a toilet, the valve is on the wall or floor behind it. For a tub or shower, there may be no local valve, and you may need the main shutoff.

If a fixture is spraying and the small valve will not turn or does not stop the flow, close the main water valve and call. Old stop valves often seize or fail to seal, and replacing them is a normal part of a visit.

What a drip tells you

Drips look the same but come from different faults. Here is a quick guide.

Where it leaksLikely causeWhat the plumber does
Spout drips when offWorn washer, seat or cartridgeReplace the internal part
Handle leaksO-rings or packingReplace seals

Under the sinkSupply line, stop valve or drainReplace the failing fitting

Hot only runs weakDebris in the cartridge or aeratorClean or replace

A faucet that drips once a second wastes thousands of gallons over a year, and a steady drip also stains the sink. Fixing it early is cheap.

Shower and tub valves

A shower valve sits behind the wall. When it fails, you may see a drip from the showerhead, a handle that will not turn or water that runs lukewarm. Many valves have a replaceable cartridge, and the plumber can usually swap it from the front.

If the valve body itself is corroded or leaking behind the wall, the repair needs access. The plumber explains whether a cut in the wall or an access panel is needed. A jetted tub or a bathtub faucet with a stuck diverter is similar, and the fix depends on the model.

A drip you can ignore. A gush you cannot. Call for either.

Supply lines and stop valves

Braided supply lines burst more often than people expect, especially after about ten years. A line that bulges or has a rusty fitting is on its way. Replace supply lines when you replace a faucet, and consider replacing old stop valves at the same time.

A burst supply line under a kitchen sink can flood a cabinet and a floor in minutes. Closing the stop valve is quick if it works. Test your stop valves once a year by turning them gently, so you know they close.

Kitchen and bathroom sink drains

Slow sinks are a drain problem, not a faucet problem. Our drain cleaning page covers those. A sink that leaks from the drain tailpiece, the trap or the disposal connection is a separate repair that the plumber handles at the visit. If the leak is near a disposal, see garbage disposal repair.

Outdoor spigots and freeze protection

A hose bib that drips inside the wall in winter is a sign of freeze damage. If the spigot has a hose attached in cold weather, water can freeze and split the pipe inside the wall. Disconnect hoses in autumn and consider a frost-proof sillcock.

A split spigot may not leak until spring when the pipe thaws, then it leaks inside the wall. If you see a wet wall near an outdoor spigot, the plumber may need to repair it. See burst pipe repair for the larger repair path.

Faucet replacement and upgrades

Replacing a faucet is an easy way to update a kitchen or bath. Buy a faucet that fits the sink holes, and the plumber installs it with new supply lines. A pull-down kitchen faucet needs enough space under the sink for the hose, and a touchless faucet needs a power source.

Cheap faucets save money at purchase and cost more in repairs. Ask the plumber which brands have parts available locally.

Local note on Middletown water and fixtures

New Jersey American Water's Coastal North system lists total hardness from 72 to 140 ppm in 2023. Moderately hard water leaves white scale on aerators and showerheads, and it can clog cartridges. Cleaning aerators every few months helps, and a plumber can swap the cartridge when scale has done damage.

For permit and code questions on larger bathroom or kitchen work, ask the Middletown construction office at 1 Kings Highway. Plumbing in New Jersey must be done by a licensed master plumber.

Fixtures and their connections are installed to the standard the state adopts, the National Standard Plumbing Code 2021, adopted at N.J.A.C. 5:23-3.15.

Fixtures that point to bigger problems

A faucet that sputters or runs weak in several rooms can mean low pressure in the supply, which is covered on the water line repair page. Rental owners in Red Bank often replace stop valves at the same time as the faucet to prevent a flood.
