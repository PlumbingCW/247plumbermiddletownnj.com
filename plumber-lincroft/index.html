Where Lincroft sits

Lincroft is an unincorporated community in the southwest corner of Middletown Township, and the Census Bureau counts it as a census-designated place. It borders Holmdel Township to the west and the Swimming River Reservoir to the south. The Garden State Parkway forms its northeast edge, with Exits 114 and 109 serving it. Its ZIP code is 07738.

What the census tells us, and what it does not

The 2020 census counted 7,060 people and 2,509 housing units in Lincroft, with 3.3 percent of units vacant. The population grew 15.1 percent from 6,135 in 2010. Brookdale Community College is here, and the 228-acre Brookdale Farm Historic District was added to the National Register of Historic Places in 2020.

Those figures describe the community as a whole. They do not tell us the age of any one house, so we do not guess at pipe materials. A plumber should look at your actual system before recommending a repair.

Plumbing jobs homeowners here call about

Lincroft homes use the same fixtures and systems as the rest of the township, so the common calls look familiar:

  • Drain cleaning for slow sinks, tubs and showers
  • Water heater repair or water heater replacement when hot water fails
  • Leak detection when a water bill jumps for no clear reason
  • Toilet repair for running or leaking tanks

Three things to find out today

  • Where your main water shutoff is, so you can close it in seconds during a leak.
  • Whether your home is on public sewer or a septic system. Sewer customers in the township connect through the Township of Middletown Sewerage Authority, and the answer changes what a backup means.
  • How old your water heater is. The tag on the tank usually shows when it was made.

Permits and the licensing rule

New Jersey requires plumbing work to be performed by a licensed master plumber, and the state plumbing code is the National Standard Plumbing Code 2021, adopted at N.J.A.C. 5:23-3.15. A water heater replacement often needs a permit. The Middletown construction office at 1 Kings Highway, 732-615-2106, can tell you what your job needs. Ask any plumber for licence details before work starts.
